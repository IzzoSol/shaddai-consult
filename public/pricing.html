What you pay for

  • Calls to the hosted gateway, which meters usage and gives you a usage view. This is the part that bills per call.
  • Seats, so each person on a team has their own Claude host connected.
  • Consulting: discovery, setup and knowledge-base design, quoted per engagement.

The free local MCP stays free. It doesn't meter usage, so paid plans are for the hosted gateway and consulting.
